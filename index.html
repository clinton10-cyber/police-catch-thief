How to play

Five identical players, you and four NPCs. Every round the folded papers are reshuffled:

👑 King 1000👸 Queen 800🎩 Minister 600👮 Police 500🦹 Thief 0

Only you can see your own role — hold your card to peek. The Police steps forward and shoots who they think is the Thief.

  • Right guess → Police banks 500
  • Wrong guess → the player shot takes the 500

As Police: tap a player to lock on, tap again (or FIRE) to shoot. Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom. Keys: Space next · M mute · R camera.
