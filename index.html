How to play

Five identical players sit in a circle: You and four NPCs. Every round the folded papers are reshuffled:

King 1000 · Queen 800 · Minister 600 · Police 500 · Thief 0

Only you see your own role. The Police walks forward and shoots the player they think is the Thief.

  • Right guess → Police banks 500.
  • Wrong guess → the player shot takes the 500.

When you're Police: move to aim, tap to fire. Drag the background to look around. Roles are revealed after every shot — learn the NPCs.
